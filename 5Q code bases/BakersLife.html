🥐 Baker's Dozen - 13 Special Recipes

1. Chocolate Fudge Cake

2 cups flour
1 cup cocoa powder
1½ cups sugar
2 eggs
Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.

2. Blueberry Muffins

2 cups flour
1 cup blueberries
¾ cup sugar
1 egg
Bake at 375°F for 20 minutes.

3. Cinnamon Rolls

3 cups flour
1 cup milk
2 teaspoons cinnamon
½ cup sugar
Bake at 350°F for 25 minutes.

4. Lemon Pound Cake

2 cups flour
1 cup butter
1 cup sugar
2 tablespoons lemon juice
Bake at 350°F for 50 minutes.

5. Red Velvet Cupcakes

2 cups flour
1 cup sugar
2 eggs
2 tablespoons cocoa
Bake at 350°F for 20 minutes.

6. Apple Pie

6 apples
2 cups flour for crust
1 cup sugar
1 teaspoon cinnamon
Bake at 375°F for 45 minutes.

7. Banana Bread

3 bananas
2 cups flour
1 cup sugar
2 eggs
Bake at 350°F for 55 minutes.

8. Strawberry Cheesecake

2 cups cream cheese
1 cup strawberries
1 cup sugar
1 graham cracker crust
Bake at 325°F for 45 minutes.

9. Carrot Cake

2 cups flour
2 cups carrots
1 cup sugar
2 eggs
Bake at 350°F for 35 minutes.

10. Chocolate Brownies

1 cup cocoa
1 cup flour
1 cup sugar
2 eggs
Bake at 350°F for 25 minutes.

11. Vanilla Donuts

2 cups flour
1 cup milk
1 egg
1 teaspoon vanilla
Bake at 375°F for 15 minutes.

12. Coconut Macaroons

3 cups coconut
1 cup sugar
2 egg whites
1 teaspoon vanilla
Bake at 325°F for 20 minutes.

13. Raspberry Tart

1 pastry crust
2 cups raspberries
½ cup sugar
1 tablespoon lemon juice
Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
